GT-R R35 cockpit interior assets — provenance
==============================================

Assets in this directory
------------------------
- r35_dash_forward.png — forward dash billboard texture.
  Derived (cropped, mirrored, colour-graded, resized) by the deterministic
  script tools/r35_prepare_dash_texture.py from the user-supplied reference
  photograph:
    reference/File_The_interior_of_Nissan_GT-R_Premium_Edition_2017_year_model_at_Ni.jpg
  (fallback sources in the same pipeline:
    reference/File_Nissan_GT-R_Premium_edition_T-Spec__4BA-R35__interior.jpg.jpg,
    reference/nc_12_Nissan-GT-R-2017-Interior.fdea9e67.jpg)

- reference/*.jpg — real Nissan GT-R R35 interior photographs supplied by the
  project owner (the "user") as the texture source for the cockpit rework.
  They are kept here because .vision/ is transient/gitignored and the texture
  pipeline must remain reproducible.
