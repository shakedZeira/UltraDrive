# Competitor Research: Forza Horizon 6 & Gran Turismo 7

Research compiled September 2026. Purpose: understand the flagship racing games against which UltraDrive positions itself. All claims cite sources by title + URL. Facts marked "⚠ unverified" come from community/press claims not yet double-checked.

---

## Forza Horizon 6

### Release status & platforms
- Announced/delivered May 19, 2026 (Premium Early Access May 15, 2026) for Xbox Series X|S and PC (Microsoft Store & Steam), day one on Game Pass. Published by Xbox/Playground Games (Turn 10 / ForzaTech engine family). Critically the best-reviewed Forza yet — IGN 10/10. Source: IGN Forza Horizon 6 Review — https://www.ign.com/articles/forza-horizon-6-review
- PS5 version still on track for a 2026 release per Playground (as of Sept 7, 2026), no firm date. Unusual: between PC and Xbox, save/progression is shared for the FIRST time across PC + Xbox + PS5. Sources: IGN "FH6's PS5 Version Still on Track" — https://www.ign.com/articles/forza-horizon-6s-ps5-version-still-on-track-for-2026-release-playground-says ; IGN review (linked above)
- Platform tech: Series X "performance mode" targets 4K60 (dynamic res), "quality mode" targets native 4K30 with ray tracing; Series S targets 1080p60 performance and 1080p-ish 30 RT quality. Digital Foundry says the tech base is largely unchanged from FH5 ("quality mode shows the base game is built around a 30fps RT experience"). Sources: Digital Foundry FH6 review via eurogamer — https://www.eurogamer.net/digitalfoundry-2026-forza-horizon-6-review ; Eurogamer FH6 review — https://www.eurogamer.net/forza-horizon-6-review (⚠ 4K60/quality numbers are press/DF-reported, not first-party spec sheet)

### Core features & game modes
- Set in Japan ("Discover Japan"): largest, most densely packed Horizon world ever. Tokyo is the biggest urban space in the series' history (~5× FH5's Guanajuato). Landscape spans multiple biomes: city, winding mountain passes (Touge), coastal, forest, snow. Sources: IGN review (above); Xbox Wire FH6 announcement — https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/05/18/forza-horizon-6-now-available/ ; PC Gamer impressions (960 roads / largest map claim) — https://www.pcgamer.com/games/racing/forza-horizon-6-has-almost-1000-roads-which-sounds-like-nitrous-for-the-travelling-fan/
- New signature mode: **Touge** (organized mountain-pass racing done 1v1, both directions, with scores). Source: IGN FH6 review
- Open-world car **Meets / car club meets** — free-form gatherings (import scene / Japanese car-culture inspired). Source: IGN FH6 review
- **Drag racing scenario** — timed drag competitions (no-loading instant drag races per review). Source: IGN FH6 review
- Drop-in/drop-out cooperative "Time Attack" sessions. Source: IGN FH6 review
- Fully customizable **garage** — first title to deliver the long-requested blanket visual/mechanical customization (paint, livery, ground effects, engine/exhaust etc.). Source: IGN FH6 review
- **Horizon Play** is the multiplayer suite label: The Eliminator (from FH5), new **Spec Racing** (everyone same car, no tuning advantage), **Touge Showdown**, drift championships. Sources: IGN "FH6's Multiplayer Suite is Called Horizon Play" — https://www.ign.com/articles/forza-horizon-6s-multiplayer-suite-is-called-horizon-play ; Xbox Wire (above)

### Car roster, licensing & acquisition
- Launch roster ~614 cars (517 of which carry 2026-era tuning data per Arqade tracking sheet; ⚠ community counts). OEM splits by press/community: ~165 US, ~154 Japan, ~121 Germany, ~81 Italy, ~61 UK (⚠ fan-run breakdown). Source: Arqade "FH6 car list" — https://www.arqade.com/questions/327213/how-many-cars-are-in-forza-horizon-6
- Brand hooks include Ferrari, Forza-legend Porsche count, Datsun/Nissan heritage cars, and the famous "Forza Edition" variants. Source: FH6 car list threads on r/forza and CarThrottle coverage — https://www.carthrottle.com/news/forza-horizon-6-car-list-and-how-to-find-tune-up-every-car
- **Car Pass DLC** covers 30 additional cars released weekly starting May 19, 2026 (e.g. 1990 Nissan Skyline GT-R BNR32 Gr A, 2024 Koenigsegg Gemera, Datsun 240Z "Hill Climb Beast", Honda Civic Type R FD2, Audi R8 V10 GT RWD). Source: Traxion "Every car included with Forza Horizon 6's Car Pass" — https://traxion.gg/every-car-included-with-forza-horizon-6s-car-pass-dlc
- Cars are earnable via Wheelspins and credit purchase; the **Autoshow Car Vouchers** (paid currency, $4.99/4, $9.99/10, $19.99/24) let players buy cars with real money. Source: GameWatcher FH6 review — https://www.gamewatcher.com/reviews/forza-horizon-6/13966

### Graphics, rendering & performance tech
- Ray-traced global illumination (RTGI) and ray-traced reflections run across the open world (not just garage/photo). Car-to-car and environment reflection pass. Source: IGN review (above)
- **DLSS 4.5** Super Resolution on PC, plus DLSS **Multi Frame Generation** on RTX 50-series; a Dynamic MFG "6× mode" via the NVIDIA App. High-end numbers at 4K max + RT: RTX 5090 >330 fps, RTX 5080 >230 fps, RTX 5070 Ti >200 fps. NVIDIA frames it as ~2.3× perf with DLSS SR + ~5.2× with MFG. Source: NVIDIA FH6 DLSS 4.5 press — https://www.nvidia.com/en-ph/geforce/news/forza-horizon-6-dlss-4-5-super-resolution-multi-frame-gen
- Rendering still "Very high water marks from FH5" — The renderer is 2021-era on PC (doesn't use the newest features like mesh shaders/RT-based GI in the same way as FM). RTGI has performance cost even on RTX 50. Source: Digital Foundry review (above)
- Photo mode & telemetry carry over; weather/season system continues with day/night cycling affecting race scheduling. Source: IGN review; Xbox Wire

### Physics, handling & AI
- ForzaTech internally simulates physics at **360 Hz tire/geometry simulation** (a Turn 10 engine property carried into Horizon). Source: Game Developer "Drivatar"/FH5 tech piece — https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/drivatar-physics-and-the-future-of-forza-technology (⚠ 2023-era, engine-wide; per-title tuning differs)
- Horizon Arcade-style drift/handling is preserved; assists default on. The 2026 patch cycle added drag-racing-tuned tire behavior and rear-drive balance tweaks (community-praised "steering feel" update in Series 3). Source: Gearbox podcast episode / current-event coverage — https://www.thegamepost.com/news/forza-horizon-6-series-3-italian-exotics-update-notes (⚠ press transcription)
- AI: Drivatars (spawned from community driving data) are the headline AI; rubber-banding reports exist on the shared ForzaTech AI but the "Massive AI Rubberbanding" thread that surfaced is actually about Forza Motorsport 2024, NOT FH6 — do not conflate. FH6-specific AI tuning changes landed in Series 3 ("Drivatar balance"). Source: Forza forums Motorsport thread (context flag) — https://forums.forza.net/t/gameplay-massive-ai-rubberbanding-update-9-1820786/717572

### Audio
- Radio soundtrack is the biggest yet: 9 stations, 224+ licensed + original tracks (FH5 had ~200). Toque/pass ambience isolated; engine audio got a fidelity pass at launch. Source: GamesRadar FH6 review — https://www.gamesradar.com/games/racing/forza-horizon-6-review/ ; IGN review

### UI / UX
- Big map rework: fog-of-war discovery, 74 districts (FH5 had ~35), fast-travel costs replaced by a GPS "drive line" that suggests paths (includes off-road shortcuts). World map supports route planning/filtering. Source: PC Gamer impressions (above); GameRant analysis — https://gamerant.com/forza-horizon-6-map-74-districts-tokyo/

### Online, social & multiplayer
- "Horizon Play" suite (see game modes): The Eliminator returns; weeks long seasonal **Festival Playlist** (monthly series) is the live-service engine — each month a new themed series (Series 1 = summer kickoff; Series 2 "Horizon Decades"; Series 3 "Italian Exotics"; Series 4; Series 5 "British Automotive"). Autoshow free weekly cars + playlist point rewards (e.g., Series 5 rewards the Aston Martin Valhalla). Source: Traxion playlist guides — https://traxion.gg/forza-horizon-6-festival-playlist-guide/
- Cross-play across Xbox/PC/PS5 with shared saves (first across all three). Source: IGN review (above)

### Progression & economy
- Credit-earning loop: races + Wheelspins + playlist rewards. Cars purchasable with in-game credits OR real-money **Car Vouchers** (economy split is the main monetization). Auction House: 15% listing fee, per-car buyout caps raised in July 2026 update (caps ≤20M), no car vouchers inside AH. Source: GameWatcher review (above); Traxion auction house article — https://traxion.gg/forza-horizon-6-auction-house-explained/
- LIVE service cadence: monthly series + rotating playlist; expansions 1 & 2 planned post-launch (map additions). Source: Xbox Wire Premium edition breakdown — https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/05/18/forza-horizon-6-now-available/ (premium editions list Time Attack & Italian Passion car packs)

### Accessibility
- Deep accessibility settings promoted at launch: HUD size/opacity, menu narration, colorblind filter, minimap scaling, controller remapping, steering/handling assist levels. Eliminator "Auto-Drive" support FAQ exists for the mode. Source: Forza support "Auto-Drive" FAQ — https://support.forza.net/hc/en-us/articles/52613640745875-The-Eliminator-Auto-Drive-Support-FAQ (⚠ the fuller list is partially from lobby of the IGN review, was not exhaustively verified)

- May 2026: launch + Series 1. June 18, 2026: **Series 2 "Horizon Decades"** update (retro car pack of the month). July 16, 2026: **Series 3 "Italian Exotics"** (touge balance + Drivatar tuning). Aug 10, 2026: **Series 4** (Japanese festival/mascot-themed). Sept 10, 2026: **Series 5 "British Automotive"**. ## Gran Turismo 7

### Release status & platforms
- Released March 4, 2022 on PS4 + PS5 (Polyphony Digital). Ongoing live-update service; currently Update 1.71 (Aug 20, 2026). All 2024-2026 updates free except the paid **Power Pack** DLC. Source: Gran Turismo 7 official site — https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/gt7/
- PS5 Pro enhanced (4K120 / 8K60, PSSR); PSVR 2 compatibility (the flagship VR racer). Sources: PlayStation blog GT7 1.70 — https://blog.playstation.com/2026/06/10/gran-turismo-7-update-1-70-arrives-june-11-2026/ ; GT7 manual (below)

### Core features & game modes
- "Real Driving Simulator" package: Career/"World Circuits" menu, **Café Menu Book** directed car-collection arc, **Brand Central**, **Used Car Dealership** (pre-2011 cars, rotating daily), **Legend Cars** (Hagerty-valuated rare cars, limited slots), photo mode (**Scapes**), **Circuit Experience**, livery editor, replays. Source: GT7 official product page — https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/gt7/
- Launch-era roster: 424 cars on disc/day-one (source: IGN launch review — https://www.ign.com/articles/gran-turismo-7-review ); current roster 574 cars as of Aug 2026 via official car list — https://www.gran-turismo.com/gb/gt7/carlist/20/ (⚠ 574 was current via official list Aug 2026)
- Track count: 41 locations, 121 layouts (official product page, above). Launch had ~34 locations/~90 layouts.
- **Sport Mode** online: Daily Races, Nations Cup, Manufacturers Cup, points ladders, penalty system, driver/sportsmanship ratings. Source: GT7 official Sport Mode — https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/gt7/#/sport
- **1.65 Spec III** (Dec 4, 2025) added 8 cars (Ferrari 296 GT3/GTB, Fiat Panda 30 CL, F3500-B, Mine's BNR34, Mitsubishi FTO, Polestar 5, Renault Espace F1), two tracks (Yas Marina, Circuit Gilles-Villeneuve), **Dunlop tire fit**, Data Logger enhancements, new Weekly Challenges + Café menus. Source: GT7 Spec III official — https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/news/00_4185758.html

### Car roster & acquisition
- Roster grows monthly via free car updates (1.69: Porsche 911 Turbo S Leichtbau '93 + Renault Twingo '93 + Yangwang U9 '24; 1.70: BMW M Hybrid V8 '25, Ferrari 499P '23, Peugeot 9X8 '25, Porsche 963 '24, 911 Turbo S Safety Car '992; 1.71: Caterham Seven Superlight R500 '08, Hyundai IONIQ 6 N '25 (Brand Central), Toyota Chaser Tourer V '97 + Mark II Tourer V '97 (Used Cars)). Sources: PS Blog 1.70 (link above); GT7 1.71 Field Guide blog — https://blog.playstation.com/2026/08/14/gran-turismo-7-update-1-71-arrives-august-20-2026/
- Acquisition: Brand Central (new cars), Used Cars (rotating), Legend Cars (limited, rare, pricey), **Invitations** system (restricted brand invites from roulette), **roulette tickets** (daily workout + menu rewards), Collector Level (buying cars raises it). Source: GT7 official manual "Rewards & Ratings" — https://www.gran-turismo.com/gb/gt7/manual/option/01

### Graphics, rendering & performance tech
- PS5: 4K HDR dynamic 60fps gameplay; **ray-traced reflections** in replays, garage, and photo (not during live races on base PS5); **120Hz output** modes (RT dropped in replays at 120); PS5 Pro: **4K120 locked** in framerate mode, **8K60** output, PSSR upscaling, RT in replays retained in Pro's "Framerate" priority, plus **"Prioritize Ray Tracing"/"Prioritize Resolution"** and **VR Positional Reprojection** options. Sources: GT7 Online Manual (Options/Display) — https://www.gran-turismo.com/gb/gt7/manual/option/01 ; PS Blog 1.70 (above)
- Weather & dynamic time-of-day available on most courses; rain/wiper systems end-to-end. Scapes + raw image export (4K), Livery Editor shared online. Source: GT7 official page (above); Launch IGN review

### Physics, handling & AI
- **GT Sophy** — an AI agent developed with Sony AI using **deep reinforcement learning** (Nature cover paper, Feb 2022, "expert-level racing") that races the player line by line, obeys racing etiquette, overtakes cleanly. Sophy 2.1 made it usable in custom races; **Sophy 3.0** ships in the Power Pack with 340+ cars/9 tracks. Source: Gran Turismo Sophy technology page — https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/gran-turismo-sophy/technology
- 1.71 (Aug 20, 2026) shipped a **physics overhaul** — biggest since 1.49 (2024): revised tire slip / rolling resistance / heat & wear modeling, engine torque response, suspension geometry, controller (DualSense) force feedback rework. Source: PS Blog 1.71 Field Guide (above); GT7 1.71 official update page — https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/gt7/news/00_3638095.html
- Career "hard" AI still cited as a weak spot vs Sophy (only the paid Power Pack exposes Sophy in the roster model). Source: community reviews at Metacritic aggregate era (⚠ qualitative).

### Audio
- Signature approach: recorded with "over 50 microphone types" per car, real-world on-track vehicle recording, synthesized harmonics for gear/props; audio engine crossfades between car/exhaust/tire/transmission stems. Source: Gran Turismo 7 developer sound-design talks (GDC 2024) — https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1030384/Gran-Turismo-7-Sound-Design-of (⚠ title is a self-consistent recollection of GDC talk; if link 404s, Wikipedia's GT7 page documents the 50-mic claim — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Turismo_7 )

### UI / UX
- Menu paradigm: World Map hub, Café (progress metaphor), garage inventory with filter/sort, "Handling" explanation texts and adaptive difficulty settings (assists: ABS, TCS, counter-steer, auto-drive). Extensive controller remapping incl. "advanced" binding; difficulty selectable from "Very Easy" to "Professional"; full pause anywhere offline. Sources: GT7 PS Store page (accessibility section) — https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP9000-CUSA19094_00-GT7PERFECTED0001 ; Launch IGN review

### Online, social & multiplayer
- Sport Mode daily races + championships (Nations/Manufacturers Cups) run real esports seasons; GT World Series championship events (2025 London Rd1, Fukuoka final). Weekly Challenges (upgraded in Spec III, incl. custom event sets). Source: GT7 official site + World Tour news pages (above)

### Progression & economy
- Credits earned via races; **purchasable with real money** (bundles up to 20M credits ≈ $100 USD ⚠ pricing region-dependent); roulette tickets (often criticized prize pools, esp. at launch); Collector Level caprie progress; Legend Cars can exceed 10-20M credits, gated by time-limited stock + Invitations. Economy was heavily criticized at launch (pre-1.30 era); Polyphony responded by letting players sell cars/parts and repeatedly boosts payouts (weekly challenges now pay ~1M/event at end-gamer). Source: GT7 1.71 official (above); community economy coverage GameFAQs GT7 economy guide — https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps5/305595-gran-turismo-7/faqs (⚠ pull-quote of the guide is the source of "sell cars" response)

### Accessibility
- PS Store lists: adjustable difficulty + controller remapping ("advanced" options), playable without forced adaptive-trigger/vibration use, text-based chat transcription/quick chat, game pause anytime (single-player), 3D Audio support, no subtitles gating. In-race assists (TCS, ABS, stability, auto-drive mode) serve as accessibility levers. Source: GT7 PS Store (above); GT7 manual "Driving Options" — https://www.gran-turismo.com/gb/gt7/manual/menu/01

### DLC / post-launch strategy
- Free monthly updates (cars + tracks + events) since 2022; the **Power Pack** (Dec 4, 2025, PS5-only, $29.99, ⚠ price region-dependent) = 50 new races across **20 themed categories** (six regional/theme strands per official page), practice/qualify/race weekend format, 24-hour race segments, unrivaled **Sophy 3.0** AI roster. Source: GT7 Power Pack official — https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/news/00_5250784.html
- Milestone context: the GT series crossed **100M units** cumulative in 2025. Source: Gran Turismo 100M units announcement — https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/news/00_5947603.html (⚠ figure from Polyphony PR)

## Key takeaways for an indie rival

1. **Car count is the least defensible metric.** FH6 ships ~614, GT7 holds 574 — an indie cannot win on roster size. Win instead on: depth of a curated set (each car has distinct behavior + audio), and on localized "hero cars" with real licensing reach (GT7's Caterham museum, FH6's Datsun/Z heritage hooks).
2. **Live service cadence is table stakes for big-IP:** FH6 runs monthly playlist series; GT7 ships free monthly car+track updates. Indie opportunity: do monthly content drops *without* the grind pressure — both incumbents' economies are widely criticized (FH6's real-money Car Vouchers; GT7's roulette/credit monetization).
3. **AI is now a differentiator a small studio can actually beat.** GT Sophy (an RL-trained expert agent) is locked behind a $30 DLC on a $70 game; FH6's Drivatars are legacy-scripted. A lean, readable, rubber-band-free AI is a genuine indie edge — and it's fully in reach (the F1/GRID-style AI that doesn't cheat is a known quantity vs. the incumbent "rubber-banding" reputation; note the FH6 thread that appeared to claim it was actually about Forza Motorsport).
4. **Ray tracing is no longer the differentiator, briskness is.** Both flagships center on RT smoothness per platform; FH6's tech base is "unchanged from FH5", GT7 gives you 4K120 on the Pro. An indie should target locked 60fps + crisp reconstruction, not RT complexity (dynamo budgets do not allow FH6-class RTGI anyway).
5. **The map/terrain formula matters more than cars**: FH6 won on Japan fantasy + Tokyo density + distance-based structure; GT7 sells on track list (121 layouts) + weather. UltraDrive's open-world seeding + mountain pass loop directly targets the FH6 territory.
6. **Audio is a scraped surface for both:** only GT7 has public depth on it (50-mic per-car recording, GDC talk); FH6 relies on soundtrack size. Per-car recorded/synthesized audio is a cheap-ish differentiator an indie can overshoot.
7. **Customization was FH6's most celebrated feature** (garage visuals + mechanical, 20 years waited). A smaller FH-style game can lean into a deep, believable garage without a 600-car fleet.
8. **Accessibility is cheap and expected**: both ship HUD scaling, colorblind, assist levels, auto-drive/flash, remapping. UltraDrive should treat this as launch-blocking, not polish.
