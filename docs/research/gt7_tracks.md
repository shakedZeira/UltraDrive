# Gran Turismo 7 — Complete Track (Circuit) List

Research compiled 2026-10-13 from the Gran Turismo Wiki (gran-turismo.fandom.com,
GT7 Track List page, which cites the official gran-turismo.com tracklist) plus
infobox data for individual tracks.

**Scale of the roster (as of game version v1.72, September 2026):**

- **~41 environments** (track locations)
- **84 distinct layouts** (each environment has 1–6 layouts, e.g. Nürburgring has 6)
- **~120 total configurations** when reverse layouts are included
- **+1 upcoming:** SUGO International Racing Course (Spec IV, first half, releases
  2026-10-15) — not yet playable, listed separately at the end.

**Mixed real/fictional roster:** the majority are faithfully laser-scanned real-world
circuits (Monza, Spa, Le Mans, Nordschleife, Mount Panorama, Suzuka, Fuji, Interlagos,
Daytona, Watkins Glen, Yas Marina, …). A large minority are **GT-original fictional
tracks** — many dating back to GT1–GT4 and returning here remastered or redesigned
(Deep Forest, Trial Mountain, Grand Valley, High Speed Ring, Route X, plus newer
originals like Alsace, Lago Maggiore, Sainte-Croix, Dragon Trail, Eiger Nordwand,
Kyoto Driving Park, Sardegna, Blue Moon Bay, Broad Bean, Northern Isle, Colorado
Springs, Fisherman's Ranch). Fictional tracks carry an in-game country/region flag
(e.g. Deep Forest = Switzerland, Trial Mountain = USA).

**Data confidence note:** all **lengths** below are the exact figures from the wiki.
**Corner counts marked "~" and elevation figures** are approximations from established
circuit knowledge, not verified per-track wiki infobox data — treat them as indicative.
1 mi = 1.609 km.

**Direction:** all real-world circuits run their real-world racing direction (mostly
clockwise). Exceptions: Daytona road course (CCW), Circuit Gilles-Villeneuve (CCW),
Mount Panorama (CCW), Yas Marina (CCW). Ovals (Daytona Tri-Oval, Blue Moon Bay,
Northern Isle) run left-turn in NASCAR style. **Almost every layout in GT7 is
reversible in-game** (the reverse variant is offered as a separate configuration and
is counted in the "~120 configurations" figure); Tokyo Expressway is the special case,
shipping explicit CW and CCW layouts as distinct entries with different lengths.

## Per-track highlights (elevation character + notable features)

Corner counts marked ~ are approximations. Elevation figures are from circuit
infoboxes where verified (marked ✓), otherwise indicative.

### Europe (real circuits)

**Autodromo Nazionale Monza — Italy · 5,793 m · CW**
The "Temple of Speed." Almost flat (✓ ~11 m elevation change), the fastest circuit in
GT7: long flat-out blasts broken by heavy braking zones. ~11 turns. Longest straight
~1.2 km (Rettifilo). Chicanes added after the old 5.793 km no-chicane flow — GT7
offers both configurations. Rain + GT Sophy supported.

**Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps — Belgium · 7,044 m · CW**
The longest active F1 circuit and the largest elevation change of any European track
in the game (✓ ~102 m). ~19–20 turns. Iconic Eau Rouge/Raidillon blind uphill left-
right-left (~40 m climb in one sweep) onto the Kemmel Straight. The 24h variant adds
night/stint config. Rain + GT Sophy supported.

**Circuit des 24 Heures du Mans — France · 13,629 m · CW**
The longest track in GT7 by a wide margin. Endurance icon: the ~1.3+ km Mulsanne
Straight (with two chicanes in the modern layout; GT7 also ships a no-chicane
variant), the Porsche Curves, Indianapolis/Arnage complex, Ford chicane. Flat to
gently rolling (~5–10 m). Both configurations support rain + GT Sophy — the
flagship endurance experience.

**Nürburgring — Germany · Nordschleife 20,832 m · 24h Combined 25,378 m · CW**
The largest track in the game. ~70+ named corners/bends, ~300 m of total climb and
descent across a lap. Legendary features: the banked Caracciola Karussell, the
Fuchsröhre compression, Flugplatz crests, Döttinger Höhe straight (combined layout).
Six configurations: Nordschleife, 24h, 24h Combined, Nordschleife+Döttinger Höhe,
Sprint (5,148 m), Sprint Short (3,629 m). All rain + GT Sophy supported. The
definitive "endurance/Nordschleife bucket-list" track.

**Red Bull Ring — Austria · 4,318 m · CW**
Formerly A1-Ring/Osterreichring, set in Styrian hills: ~65 m elevation change,
~10 turns. Steep uphill into the Remus/Schlossgold hairpins, fast downhill through
Rauch and Rindt. Short course 2,336 m. Rain + GT Sophy supported.

**Brands Hatch — United Kingdom · GP 3,946 m / Indy 1,944 m · CW**
Classic natural-amphitheatre British circuit in the Kent woods. GP layout ~13 turns
with the plunging Druids hairpin and the fast Hawthorns/Westerfield section; Indy is
the short club circuit (~6 corners) famous for F1 sprints. Modest elevation (~30–40 m).

**Goodwood Motor Circuit — United Kingdom · 3,809 m · CW**
Historic post-WWII airfield circuit, home of the Revival: extremely fast and flat
(~10 m), only ~7 corners, minimal run-off — punishes mistakes, rewards commitment.
Unique atmosphere piece in the roster.

**Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya — Spain · 4,655 m (GP) / 4,730 m (2021) / 2,977 m (National) / 1,133 m (Rallycross) · CW**
The F1 testing workhorse. ~16 turns, ~30 m elevation: long front straight, the
long Turn 3 right-hander, technical final sector. The 2021 layout re-profiled the
final chicane area; National cuts the last sector; the Rallycross layout (1,133 m,
dirt/jump) is one of only two rallycross tracks in the game.

### North America (real circuits)

**Daytona International Speedway — USA · Road 4,023 m (CCW) / Tri-Oval 5,729 m · Tri-oval runs left-turn**
The "World Center of Racing." The road course runs CCW inside/outside the 31°-banked
oval: 12 turns including the tight International Horseshoe and the Kink, plus
high-speed superspeedway sections at full banking. Tri-Oval config is pure NASCAR
looping. Virtually flat (~1 m).

**Watkins Glen International — USA · Full (Boot) 5,423 m / Short 3,942 m · CW**
Upstate New York classic. The Boot adds the downhill/off-camber Boot section;
~11 turns, ~47 m elevation change. Fast, flowing, old-school Armco-lined character;
host of the Six Hours endurance race.

**WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca — USA · 3,602 m · CW**
Famous for the **Corkscrew**: a blind crest plunging ~18 m down through a left-right
chicane in ~450 m. ~18 turns, ~55 m elevation change overall, dry California hills.

**Road Atlanta — USA · 4,088 m · CW**
Michelin Raceway road course, ~12 turns, ~38 m elevation. Characterized by the
climbing Turn 1, the esses, and the plunging Turn 12 onto the front straight under
the bridge. Home of Petit Le Mans (endurance vibes).

**Willow Springs International Raceway — USA · Big Willow 3,951 m / Streets of Willow 2,675 m (CCW) / Horse Thief Mile 1,624 m · CW (Streets = CCW)**
The oldest permanent US road course in the game, high-desert California. Big Willow
~9 fast, committing corners over rolling terrain with big elevation sweeps and almost
no run-off. Streets of Willow is a tighter, more technical counter-clockwise variant
plus a skid-pad section; Horse Thief Mile is a tiny 1.6 km gymkhana-style course.

### Asia (real circuits)

**Suzuka International Racing Course — Japan · Full 5,807 m / East 2,243 m · CW**
The only figure-8 Grand Prix circuit in the world — the crossover bridge. ~18 turns,
~40 m elevation: Degner 1/2, the long Spoon Curve, the 130R sweep, and the
S-curve complex. Rain + GT Sophy supported; a benchmark handling track.

**Fuji Speedway — Japan · Full 4,563 m / Short 4,526 m · CW**
Foot of Mount Fuji. The longest straight in Japan (~1.5 km) followed by a tight,
technical infield; ~16 turns, ~40 m elevation. Rain + GT Sophy supported.

**Autopolis — Japan · International 4,674 m / Short 3,022 m · CW**
A mountain circuit in Oita, ~18 turns with substantial elevation (~100 m+ character,
rolling terrain), fast sweeps into heavy braking zones. Rain + GT Sophy supported.

**Tsukuba Circuit — Japan · 2,045 m · CW**
The legendary compact time-attack ring: ~12 corners, near-flat (~10 m). First corner,
the Dunlop bridge esses, and the long final corner onto the straight. Small but
utterly unforgiving of sloppy lines — the classic hot-lap test. Rain + GT Sophy
supported.

### Oceania

**Mount Panorama (Bathurst) — Australia · 6,213 m · CCW**
A public road in daily life, a mountain in character: ~174 m of elevation (one of the
largest elevation tracks in GT7), ~15–17 turns. Climbs to Skyline, snakes down
through the Esses and The Dipper along a concrete-walled ridge, then unleashes the
~1.9 km Conrod Straight into The Chase chicane. Runs anti-clockwise.

### Middle East

**Yas Marina Circuit — United Arab Emirates · 5,281 m · CCW**
Abu Dhabi F1 venue, home of the F1 season-finale twilight race. Post-2021
re-profiling gives ~16 faster corners; long straights with heavy-braking hairpins,
the famous hotel-under-lit section, runs anti-clockwise. Added post-launch
(Spec III).

### South America

**Autódromo de Interlagos (José Carlos Pace) — Brazil · 4,309 m · CCW**
Senna's home track. Short, undulating (~43 m), anti-clockwise: the plunging Senna S,
the long uphill Curva do Sol onto the Reta Oposta, and the tricky Descida do Lago.
A rhythm/kerb-riding classic.

### Canada (real)

**Circuit Gilles-Villeneuve — Canada · 4,361 m · CCW**
Montreal island street-park circuit, anti-clockwise, ~14 turns, near-flat. Long
straights into heavy chicanes, famous final "Wall of Champions" corner. Home of the
F1 Canadian GP; added post-launch (Spec III).

## GT-original (fictional) tracks — the legacy roster

These are Polyphony's own designs, most returning from GT1–GT4 and remastered with
modern graphics. GT7 launched with several new original layouts and added more
post-launch.

**Deep Forest Raceway — Switzerland flag · 4,253 m · 18 turns · ✓50 m elevation · 765 m longest straight · CW**
Fan-favourite since GT1. Alpine gorge setting: tunnels, a cliff-edge back straight
with no barriers, and (in the GT7 redesign) a new downhill hairpin. The series'
signature "mountain tunnel" track alongside Trial Mountain.

**Trial Mountain — USA (Sierra Nevada) flag · 5,434 m · 15 turns · ✓58 m elevation · 1,340 m longest straight · CW**
The other GT1 legend. Two tunnels (including a long back-straight tunnel), mountain
cresting esses, the famous corkscrew-like downhill. GT7 ships a redesign with
re-profiled sections. Rain supported.

**Grand Valley — Highway 1 (Big Sur) 5,099 m / South 3,076 m · USA flag · CW**
Returning GT2-era favourite reimagined as a Pacific Coast Highway cliff road
(update 1.29): the signature bridge over the ocean, big drop-offs, ~97 m elevation
on Highway 1 with 18 turns and a 995 m straight. South is the tighter original-style
short layout.

**High Speed Ring — Japan (Hakone) flag · 4,345 m · 6 turns · ✓8.5 m elevation · 1,060 m longest straight · CW**
The GT1 "intro track": a fast 6-corner loop with banked corners and a dam/lake
setting. Simple, flat, and grippy — the game's arcade-friendly showcase circuit.
Rain supported.

**Route X — USA flag · 30,283 m · Test track**
The absurdly long (30 km) purpose-built test facility: enormous banked ovals joined
by multi-kilometre straights and giant sweepers. A top-speed playground rather than a
racing circuit.

**Alsace Village — France flag · Village 5,423 m / Test Course 2,118 m**
New for GT7. A picturesque French village circuit with heavy elevation change and
technical tight sections; Test Course is the short technical loop.

**Circuit de la Sainte-Croix — France flag · A 9,477 m / B 7,062 m / C 10,825 m**
New for GT7 and among the longest circuits in the game: sweeping lakeside road with
three huge layout variants.

**Dragon Trail — Croatia flag · Gardens 5,209 m / Seaside 4,352 m**
Returning from GT Sport. The Seaside layout's notorious barrier-lined "death chicane"
(high-speed left-right with walls) is its signature. Gardens is the greener, inland
variant (rain supported).

**Eiger Nordwand — Switzerland flag · 2,436 m**
Added in v1.49. A tight, steep mountain climb around the Eiger: heavy elevation in a
short distance, hairpins and a scenic waterfall section.

**Lago Maggiore — Italy flag · six layouts from 1,706 m to 5,809 m**
New GT7 original Italian lakeside complex: multiple configurations from a tiny
1.7 km Centre sprint to the full 5.8 km circuit.

**Kyoto Driving Park — Japan flag · Yamagiwa 4,912 m / Miyabi 1,953 m / Combined 6,846 m**
New GT7 original: a modern Japanese test park mixing wide fast sections (Yamagiwa)
with a tight traditional-feeling technical loop (Miyabi).

**Sardegna — Italy flag · Road A 5,113 m / B 3,893 m / C 2,661 m · Windmills 3,310 m**
New GT7 originals on the Mediterranean island: rolling-road tarmac courses plus the
short, windmill-lined Windmills sprint.

**Blue Moon Bay Speedway — USA flag · Infield A 3,200 m / Full 3,350 m / Infield B 2,860 m**
Oval with infield road-course sections (banked oval + tight infield), a returning
original.

**Northern Isle Speedway — USA flag · 900 m**
A tiny left-turn oval — the smallest track in the entire game.

**Broad Bean Raceway — United Kingdom flag · 1,665 m**
A small British village-style sprint course, new for GT7.

**Colorado Springs — USA flag · 2,990 m**
A high-altitude "Motor World" park circuit, new for GT7.

**Fisherman's Ranch — USA flag · 6,893 m**
The flagship rally/dirt circuit: long gravel blast through Californian ranch land
with big jumps and sliding sections — the home of GT7 rally events.

**Lake Louise — Canada flag · Long 3,068 m / Short 2,577 m / Mountain 3,694 m**
The only snow/ice tracks in the game: frozen-lake and mountain roads with genuinely
reduced grip.

**Tokyo Expressway — Japan flag · Central/East/South, CW + CCW each (4,405–7,301 m)**
Urban expressway/time-attack loops (returning from GT Sport): elevated Tokyo
highways, tight tunnels and sweeping elevated turns at dusk/night. Each zone ships
distinct CW and CCW layouts (different sectors, not mirrors).

**Upcoming — SUGO International Racing Course (Japan) · 3,586 m · 13 turns · 69.8 m elevation · 704.5 m longest straight**
Announced for GT7 Spec IV, first half, releasing **2026-10-15** — the real Japanese
circuit (home of the JGTC finale), returning to the series. Not counted in the 84
layouts above.

## Appendix — Rain & GT Sophy support

GT7 supports dynamic weather/rain on only a subset of tracks; GT Sophy (the
deep-RL AI driver) also runs on a specific subset (expanded over updates; rain +
Sophy landed together for the first six in v1.33, and the wiki's v1.72 flags mark
the following layouts):

| Track (layout) | Rain | GT Sophy |
|----------------|------|----------|
| Autopolis (both layouts) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Fuji Speedway (both) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Le Mans 24h (both configurations) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Nürburgring (all 6 layouts) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Red Bull Ring (both) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Suzuka (both) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Spa-Francorchamps (both) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Tsukuba Circuit | ✓ | ✓ |
| Dragon Trail — Gardens | ✓ | ✓ |
| High Speed Ring | ✓ | ✓ |
| Tokyo Expressway — Central CW/CCW, East CW/CCW | ✓ | ✓ |

In short: **11 environments (~22 layouts) have rain**, all of which also support
GT Sophy. The rest are dry-conditions only.
