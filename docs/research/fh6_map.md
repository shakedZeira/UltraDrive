# Forza Horizon 6 — "Horizon Japan" Open-World Map: Reference Dossier

> Research compilation for game-world / level-design reference. All figures sourced from
> press, official Forza news, and community mapping as of **Sep 2026**. Items marked ⚠ are
> **fan estimates or single-source claims that are NOT officially confirmed** — treat with
> caution and cross-check before using as design inputs.
>
> Launch context: Full map reveal 2026-04-08 (forza.net); Early access 2026-05-15; launch
> 2026-05-19 (Xbox Series X|S / PC, Game Pass day one; PS5 later in 2026).

## 1. Map Scale & Geography

### 1.1 The pitch
- Official descriptor, Playground Games: **"our most dense and vertical map yet"**, stretching
  "from the iconic downtown streets of Tokyo City all the way to the snowy Japanese Alps"
  (Forza Horizon 6 - Full Map Reveal — https://forza.net/news/forza-horizon-6-full-map-reveal).
- Art director Don Arceta: "biggest map yet", "big, but also dense… there's always something
  around the corner"; Microsoft declined to publish a km² number ("I won't put a number on it")
  (yelzkizi.org — https://yelzkizi.org/fh6-japan-map-reveal-fans-decode-huge-world-size/;
  forzahorizon6.net — https://forzahorizon6.net/fh6/size-km/).
- First mainline Horizon set in Japan; fictionalised/condensed Japan rather than 1:1 replica
  (Game Rant — https://gamerant.com/forza-horizon-6-map-size-comparison-big-small-fh4-fh5/).

### 1.2 Map size — sizes sent to compare against (⚠ community data)
**No official km² figure exists.** All of the following are community estimates:

| Game | Setting | Estimated size | Source / confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FH3 (2016) | Australia | ~64–67 km² | Game-Meta table; horizon hub — ⚠ community only |
| FH4 (2018) | UK | ~71 km² (27.4 mi²) | DonJoewonSong fan measure, widely cited |
| FH5 (2021) | Mexico | ~107 km² (41.3 mi²) | DonJoewonSong fan measure ("verified") via GhostKeys |
| FH6 (2026) | Japan | **~246 km² (~95 mi²)** total playable | ⚠ yelzkizi fan-mapping; ≈2.3× FH5's 107 km² — GhostKeys: https://ghostkeys.eu/blog/forza-horizon-6-japan-map-revealed |

Conflicting fan estimates to be aware of:
- ⚠ ~142–145 km² via a pixel-count using FH5's stadium as scale (r/ForzaHorizon,
  https://www.reddit.com/r/ForzaHorizon/comments/1sk61f9/forza_horizon_6_map_size/); same thread
  measured the area at roughly **12.3 km × 18.5 km** vs FH5's ~9.6 km × 16 km.
- ⚠ ~100 km² "drivable terrain only" (fan/Bandurart via forzahorizon6.net size page).
- ⚠ A different comparison table (fh6wiki.com) claims ~78 sq mi (~202 km²) vs FH5 ~55 sq mi and
  ~540 mi of roads vs ~380 mi — **single source, unverified** (https://fh6wiki.com/compare).

**Design takeaway:** the *Playground-endorsed* framing is **density over raw size** — "more roads,
more junctions and more POIs per square kilometre" than any prior entry. Size debates are secondary
to road/POI density in every official and most review sources (timesaver.gg —
https://timesaver.gg/blog/forza-horizon-6-map-japan-regions-landmarks).

### 1.3 Vertical layout & elevation strategy
- Structurally **a vertical axis**: Tokyo anchors the **south** at lowest elevation; Japanese Alps
  dominate the **north** at the game's highest elevation, peaks ~**3,000 m** (timesaver.gg; fh6.willgameguide.com
  — https://fh6.willgameguide.com/map-collectibles/japan-map/).
- Most vertical Horizon map: sea-level coastal roads → Tokyo's elevated expressways → alpine snow
  walls; elevated urban road networks reuse tech first built for FH5's Hot Wheels expansion
  (timesaver.gg).
- Low-mountain/highland biome is the **connective tissue** between city sprawl and the touge
  country (allthings.how — https://allthings.how/forza-horizon-6-map-every-confirmed-region-road-and-poi-in-forza-horizon-japan/).
- Reviewer observation: so much elevation + winding roads + obscured sightlines that "you'll never
  quite be able to see the whole map from any given vantage point" — openness is *concealed*, not
  revealed (TechRadar — https://www.techradar.com/gaming/forza-horizon-6-review).
- Elevation is a *driving* mechanic: TechRadar notes tracks can "start in the snowy alps and rocket
  down into rocky ravines"; Red Bull review highlights rally cars drifting "down steep mountain
  roads and into tight hairpins" and supercars past 400 kph on motorways
  (redbull.com — https://www.redbull.com/us-en/forza-horizon-6-review).

### 1.4 Biomes
Six biomes per the game.wiki resource (5 non-urban + Tokyo); press/guides commonly group as
"Tokyo + 5 rural biomes" (forzahorizon6.net):

| Biome | Character | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tokyo City | Dense urban core, ~5× FH5's Guanajuato / ~2.5× FH4's Edinburgh | Series' largest urban area; 4 districts |

| Coast | Fishing villages, beaches, clifftop roads | Izu/Hakone coastal flavour |
| Low mountains / Lowlands | Mid-elevation forested passes, rolling terrain | Touge territory start |
| Highlands | Highest non-alpine zone, hills & mountain roads | Sotoyama/Takashiro band |

Sources: game.wiki Locations — https://game.wiki/forza-horizon-6/locations (6 biomes, permanent
alpine snow, ski resort with working chairlifts); allthings.how / shigjeta.net (5 non-urban biomes +
Tokyo, elevation as structuring feature).

### 1.6 How regions connect
- **Vertical country stacking:** Tokyo (south) → plains/low-mountain connective tissue → highlands
  band → Alps (north) (allthings.how).
- A **perimeter expressway loops the entire landmass**; a bullet train line parallels much of the
  same corridor, enabling train-versus-car moments (allthings.how; PC Gamer describes Hokubu shot
  through with elevated bullet-train tracks).
- Inside Tokyo, the **C1 loop is a second, tighter urban ring**.
- **Legend Island** connects via two bridges, locked behind progression (gamingpromax).
- Named route corridors (Sada Pass, Bohashi Bridge, Kawazu Loop Bridge) stitch northern passes
  into the Alps network (allthings.how).

## 2. Road Network Design

### 2.1 Road counts
- **673 roads**, "10 roads per square" style novelty: PC Gamer preview build logged "84 of 673
  roads"; the number **held at launch** per post-launch coverage ⚠ (PC Gamer;
  GhostKeys https://ghostkeys.eu/blog/forza-horizon-6-japan-map-revealed). Red Bull's review
  loosely says "700-plus roads" (rounding / includes unclassified segments) (redbull.com).
- **76 discoverable areas** across regions per game.wiki completion tracker (https://game.wiki/forza-horizon-6/locations).
- FH5 never published a road count — the "more roads than ever" claim is qualitative ⚠ (mitchcactus
  https://mitchcactus.co/blog/forza-horizon-6/forza-horizon-6-670-roads-explained/).

### 2.2 Road hierarchy (what a road-planner should steal)
FH6's roads read as four distinct tiers, each with its own car class and event type:

1. **Urban expressways / high-speed rings**
   - **C1 Inner Loop (Tokyo)** — ~**14.8 km** elevated ring expressway; banked turns, tunnels,
     concrete canyons; marquee night-run and "most popular player-created race route"; Wangan
     Midnight / Tokyo Drift canon (timesaver.gg; gamingpromax; bossdown
     https://bossdown.com/guides/forza-horizon-6-japan-map-guide/).
   - **Shuto Expressway** — ~10+ km of elevated highway in/out of Tokyo (fh6.willgameguide.com).
   - **Wangan / Bayshore expressway** — wide, flat, fastest; "longest uninterrupted straight
     sections in the entire map"; drag strip territory, top-speed runs (gamingpromax).
   - **Perimeter expressway** around the whole landmass, paralleled by the bullet train.
2. **Touge / mountain passes** (the map's signature "soul")
   - **Mt. Haruna** (~1,449 m; the real-world basis for Initial D's Mt. Akina): tight switchbacks,
     tree-lined blind corners, guardrail-lined drops; east flank of Honshu representation
     (timesaver.gg; gamingpromax).
   - **Bandai-Azuma Skyline** (Fukushima): real-world ~28.7 km alpine route; in-game rendering is
     shorter (~6 km per one guide ⚠) but the longest high-altitude race route in the north
     (gamingpromax; fh6.willgameguide.com).
   - **Five official Touge Battle roads:** Hakone, Mt. Haruna, Bandai-Azuma, **Norikura Skyline**
     (alpine, flowing), **Arahiyama Takao Parkway** (densest forest canopy, slow/rhythm driving)
     (gamingpromax).
   - In-game length estimates ⚠ (fh6.willgameguide.com table): Akina Downhill ~5 km,
     Tateyama Alpine Route ~8 km, Hakone Turnpike ~6 km, Izu Skyline ~4 km, Norikura ~5 km,
     Bandai-Azuma ~6 km, Coastal Route 5 ~7 km, Kansai Expressway ~7 km.
3. **Coastal / scenic touring roads**
   - **Hakone Turnpike** — tight, switchback-heavy, "most technical per km" of the five
     (gamingpromax); **Izu Skyline** — flowing, panoramic, FH6's most "cinematic driving road".
   - **Rollercoaster Road** (winding elevation-change run), **Seabed Road**, beach highways in Ito.
4. **Rural / off-road lanes** — rice-field farm roads, bamboo-forest tunnels
   (Arashiyama Bamboo Forest), shrine roads, dirt paths, dead-end mountain tracks; the "final 10%"
   of discovery is hidden dirt/cross-country connectors (bossdown; allthings.how; game-meta).

### 2.3 Road-type distribution (dirt vs asphalt) — ⚠ mostly NOT officially quantified
- Dirt/off-road routes **exist and matter**: the official progression article confirms the three
  race disciplines "Road, Dirt and Cross Country Races" (forza.net —
  https://forza.net/news/forza-horizon-6-progression); TechRadar confirms dirt/cross-country
  events; GamesRadar praises the off-road feel ("the car's wheels rolling convincingly over
  smoothly undulating dirt roads") (gamesradar.com —
  https://www.gamesradar.com/games/forza-horizon/forza-horizon-6-review/).
- **No published asphalt:dirt ratio exists** for FH6 ⚠. A single-ish source (fh6wiki.com compare)
  claims "~540 mi roads" totals, but it is unverified and does not split surface types.
- Perceived mix (qualitative): tarmac dominates in Tokyo & touge; dirt concentrates in farmland
  (Minamino), cross-country cut-throughs, ski/northern routes, and coastal trails. Refs: bossdown,
  gamingpromax.

### 2.4 Elevation within the road network
- Three stacked driving planes in Tokyo: **street level → elevated expressways → tunnels**; plus
  rooftop parking structures (game-meta https://game-meta.com/forza-horizon-6-map-size-density-and-biomes-japan-open-world-breakdown/).
- Roads are "handcrafted" per preview — corners with camber, blind entries, hairpins; tech reviewers
  note roads "reward commitment and smooth throttle control" (gamingpromax).
- Mountain gradients: routes climb through elevation bands with changing road surface texture
  along Arahiyama Takao Parkway (gamingpromax).

### 2.5 Route discovery ("route line") mechanics
- **Fog of war replaces the always-on map**: first in franchise history, the full map is hidden at
  start; regions, roads, POIs reveal only as you physically drive through them. Minimap shows roads
  only after you've travelled them (totalcar/timesaver, bossdown, gamingpromax).
- **Discovered roads display white/orange; undiscovered are grey** — you can fast travel to *any
  point of any discovered road*, not just icons (xboxplay.games —
  https://xboxplay.games/forza-horizon-6/how-to-fast-travel-in-forza-horizon-6-73532).
- Completion feedback: a "Discover Japan" road track — completing a road feeds the map-completion
  / Stamp progression; ANNA (drone) overhead view reveals paths invisible from ground level
  (bossdown).
- Collectibles trigger the same proximity-reveal pattern (see §3).
- **Autodrive / cinematic travel**: set a destination and the car drives itself with a cinematic
  camera — a deliberate "tourism" mode (lootbar; player.land).

## 3. World-Manipulation Features, Traffic & Collectibles

### 3.1 Road events (event taxonomy)
- **Horizon Festival disciplines:** Road, Dirt, Cross-Country races (each with car-theme
  restrictions); after the preset run, the Race Customizer unlocks laps/weather/time/Drivatar
  count (up to 11 opponents) (forza.net progression; game.wiki road-races).
- **Street Racing** — night-time races on public roads, Tokyo-focused (game.wiki collection-journal;
  gamingpromax).
- **Touge Battles** — new 1v1 mountain-pass duels at night; 5 official routes; series first
  formalization (GhostKeys; gamingpromax).
- **Horizon Rush** — replayable timed obstacle runs (3 at launch): Tokyo City Docks, Sotoyama Ski
  Resort (Off Piste), Irokawa Space Center (Launch Control) (game.wiki horizon-rush;
  operationsports).
- **Drag Meets** — three strip venues: **Irokawa Quarter Mile**, **Ito Half Mile**, **Festival
  Kilometer** (longest) (EZG drag guide; gamingpromax).
- **Time Attack Circuits** — open-world tracks + leaderboards; **Hokubu Circuit** (S1-800 Track
  Toys); **Tsukuba Circuit short config** confirmed; **Shimanoyama Drift Circuit**;
  **Legend Island Circuit** (gamingpromax; GhostKeys; game.wiki).
- **PR Stunts** — speed traps, danger zones/jumps (TechRadar; game.wiki).
- **Showcase + Colossus** — classic cinematic Showcases; **Colossus** is the longest Goliath-type
  event in series history, a full-map freeway loop for R-class cars (game.wiki).
- **Drag/race-the-train** moments on bullet-train corridors (allthings.how).

### 3.2 Drivatars & traffic
- Drivatars are behaviour-capture AI: cornering/braking based on recorded real-player data,
  uploaded to cloud; "roads are never empty"; up to 11 Drivatars per event; offline Drivatars race
  in other players' worlds and earn credits; convoy (12-player) AI followers trail you
  (u4n.com drivatar explainer — https://www.u4n.com/index.php/news/how-drivatars-works-in-forza-horizon-6.html).
- Denser traffic vs FH5 ("more active world with denser traffic and dynamic activities") ⚠
  (lootbar.com FH6-vs-FH5 comparison).
- **Shared-world sessions of up to 72 players** on the same map ⚠ single-source (2upskill:
  https://2upskill.com/forza-horizon-6-multiplayer-guide-how-to-join-open-world-car-meets-and-convoys/).

### 3.3 Car meets / social hubs (the "alive" layer)
- **3 permanent open-world Car Meets**, joined without loading screens: **Horizon Festival Site**,
  **Okuibuki Parking Lot** (Alps), **Daikoku Parking Area** (Tokyo, JDM-iconic); + rotating
  temporary Meets on the Festival Playlist (forza.net; game.wiki car-meets; polygon).
- Some guide lists differ slightly on the spins (Tokyo = small island southeast near Drift Club
  Japan; Ohtani = Festival Kilometer drag road; Takashiro = northern border) (Polygon; WutsHot).
- **Drag Meets** (12-player, synchronized tree lights, staging lanes) and **Time Attack Circuits**
  function as informal gathering points in free roam (u4n).

### 3.4 Collectibles / bonus boards / hidden cars (exact counts)
- **400 total collectibles from launch:** **200 Regional Mascots** + **200 Bonus Boards**
  (previously "XP Boards") (PowerPyx https://www.powerpyx.com/forza-horizon-6-all-collectible-locations-treasure-map-mascots-bonus-boards/;
  games.gg; game.wiki collectibles).
- Bonus Board values reported two ways: PowerPyx says tiers 10/30/50 XP ⚠ vs BossDown saying
  1,000/3,000/5,000 XP ⚠ — same 3-tier structure (roadside / hidden / rooftop-jump), different
  denominations across sources. For a planner: **three difficulty tiers, roughly ⅓ each**, with the
  top tier requiring ramps/jumps (rooftops, cliffs, pagodas, Danger-Sign access).
- **Fast Travel Boards are removed** from FH6 (free fast travel instead) — several sources note
  players still search for non-existent boards (xboxplay.games; u4n).
- **Barn Finds: 15** ⚠ (lootbar beginner's guide), gated behind Stamp tiers; final Gold-tier find
  is a Mazda 787B (fh6.willgameguide.com). **Treasure Cars: 9** ⚠ photo-clue hunts.
- **Discovery Landmarks**: 76 discoverable areas tally (game.wiki); trigger-zone-based "ek
  stamp-rally" Collection Journal, incl. 4 season-locked entries (allthings.how discovery page).
- **Aftermarket cars** spawn on roadsides with green icons, cheaper than showroom (lootbar).
- **Treasure Map** DLC ($2.99) reveals everything instantly (PowerPyx, games.gg).

### 3.5 Weather / time-of-day zoning per region
- **Dynamic day-night:** full cycle ~60 min real time (day ~40, night ~20, golden hour ~5);
  fast-travel and events advance time (fh6.willgameguide.com time-of-day guide).
- **Regional, dynamic weather** (not FH5's per-biome fixed setup): weather changes in real time and
  differs by region — Tokyo/Osaka: clear→rain/fog; Fuji/Hakone: mist, dense fog, snow, storms;
  Hokkaido-style north: blizzards; coast: tropical rain/storms (fh6.willgameguide.com weather guide).
- ⚠ **72 "weather micro-stations"** for regional climate is a single-source claim
  (fh6.willgameguide.com Japan map page) — NOT corroborated elsewhere.
- **Impact table (design aid, ⚠ approximate):** dry 100% grip / light rain 85% / heavy rain 65% /
  fog 90% (poor visibility) / light snow 70% / heavy snow 50% / ice 30%; snow stopping distance
  2–3× (fh6.willgameguide.com weather guide).
- **Regionalised seasons**: FH4's weekly seasonal rotation returned but **per-region** — the
  mountain pass can be snowed while the coastal highway stays dry the same day (fh6wiki.com).
  Seasons rotate every Thursday (allthings.how). Winter: Alpine ice patches, reduced visibility ->
  AWD far more valuable, aggressive RWD punished (game-meta).
- Alpine biome keeps snow **year-round** regardless of season (game.wiki).

## 4. Streaming & Performance Approach (open-world composition)

The FH6 open world is the densest/most vertical the ForzaTech engine has ever streamed; the
platform story is unusually well documented:

### 4.1 Shader/load pipeline
- **Advanced Shader Delivery (ASD):** precompiled, vendor-keyed PSO caches shipped with the
  install; first-load dropped **~90 s → ~4 s** on Windows (95% reduction claim). Requires Win11
  24H2+, recent drivers, and the MS Store/Xbox app version. Falls back to 60–90 s local
  compilation otherwise (Tom's Hardware — https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/gpu-drivers/forza-horizon-6-boots-up-in-just-4-seconds-instead-of-90-with-new-advanced-shader-delivery-tech-and-amd-gpus-microsoft-claims-95-percent-reduction-in-gaming-load-times;
  SpecPicks; GameGeeker). On Steam Deck: ~45 s → ~12 s via Valve precompile ⚠ (SpecPicks).
- Runtime cache still warms for rare effects (first thunderstorm, damage states, procedural grass),
  8–20 ms spikes ⚠; steadies after ~20 min varied play (SpecPicks).

### 4.2 Streaming architecture
- **DirectStorage 1.2/1.3** with GPU-side GDeflate decompression (offloads CPU in Tokyo);
  **EnqueueRequests API** for fine-grained batching + D3D12 fence synced scheduling
  (borecraft storage findings — https://borecraft.com/findings/fh6-storage-findings.html).
- The engine's "low streaming bandwidth" error is an **I/O ceiling**, not a network issue — tile
  feeding at speed (eurogamersonline).
- ⚠ Single-source numbers: at 4K Extreme the engine can peak above **1 GB/s**; through Tokyo at
  ~400 km/h the engine may stream **~2 GB/s of assets**; 4K Extreme + Extreme RT mandate NVMe and
  24–32 GB RAM buffering (borecraft). At 1080p/1440p SATA SSDs are explicitly "fine"
  (borecraft; SpecPicks measured the FH6 shader+asset cold payload at ~2.4 GB).
- **Vertical streaming complexity** unique to FH6: multi-level parking garages, elevated highways,
  skyscrapers whose upper geometry must load for RT reflections — multi-axis (forward + camera
  tilt) asset requests, heavy occlusion-culling cycles in the urban jungle (borecraft).

### 4.3 World composition / fast travel
- **Free fast travel to any discovered road point** (no credits, no Fast-Travel Boards, no player
  house requirement) — the most permissive fast travel in series history; events can teleport too
  (xboxplay.games; player.land).
- **Load-free drag events:** Drag Meets and Car Meets live *inside* the shared open world — you
  drive in, sync, race; no lobby/menu seams; convoy members get pulled into the same activity
  ("fully seamless") (2upskill; game.wiki car-meets; u4n).
- **24–25 of the map described as live-updating weather/biome states** ⚠ (rounding from
  daily-change sources) and seasons rotate weekly.

### 4.4 Platform/perf notes
- 1440p high DLSS-Q ~64 fps avg / 52 1% low on a midrange rig; VRAM ~9.6 GB mid-mission
  (SpecPicks). Ultra textures ate 11.2 GB VRAM at 1440p ⚠ (BetterFPS).
- Terrain + world streaming is tile-based and continuous ("streams large terrain tiles
  continuously") — NVMe stays the recommended drive for high-speed traversal pop-in (SpecPicks;
  eurogamersonline).

## 5. What Makes FH6's Map Feel "Alive" (vs earlier FH)

1. **Density is the product, not size.** "Almost every road feels handcrafted — hidden shortcuts,
   layered highways, alleyways, mountain tunnels, parking meet spots, drifting zones"
   (game-meta). This is the deliberate counter to FH5's long empty desert transits.
2. **Fog-of-war makes discovery a core loop**, not a checklist: regions reveal by driving; roads
   grey→white as you pass; fast travel is the reward for covering ground (bossdown; xboxplay.games).
   "The map is really a network of iconic runs rather than a single flat plane" (timesaver.gg).
3. **Event clustering by driving culture, not by grid.** Drifters gather on mountain passes and
   industrial zones, street racers on elevated highways, drag culture at staging lanes, off-road
   players in rural/cross-country regions, show-car groups at parkings (2upskill; game-meta).
4. **Open-world social anchors** — no-loading Car Meets (Daikoku, Festival Site, Okuibuki),
   12-player Drag Meets, convoys — pull players into real places (forza.net; polygon).
5. **Activities integrated into free roam** — the world hands you events organically
   (short time trial, spontaneous touge duel, player encounter) without menu-blasting
   (player.land).
6. **Regional identity per region** — 9–10 named regions each with distinct road behavior and
   climate; "every region delivers distinct driving character" (GhostKeys; redbull).
7. **Verticality as spectacle + friction** — you can never see the whole map from one vantage;
   alpine danger (ice, blizzards) changes effective route choice by season (TechRadar; game-meta).
8. **Live world rhythm** — weekly regional seasons, rotating Car Meets, day/night shorter than
   real-time, weather that alters grip/visibility tables per region (fh6.willgameguide.com;
   allthings.how).
